Company Travel and Expense Policy
Version: 2026.1
Effective date: March 1, 2026

Flights
Economy class is the standard class for domestic travel and international trips of less than six hours. Business class may be approved for international travel exceeding six hours when required by business needs.

Hotels
The standard reimbursement limit for hotel accommodation is USD 180 per night before applicable taxes. Exceptions require advance approval from the employee's department head.

Meals
Employees traveling for business may claim up to USD 60 per day for meals. Individual meal receipts should be retained where required by the expense system.

Ground Transportation
Reasonable taxi, rideshare, rail, and public transportation expenses are reimbursable when incurred for business travel.

Expense Submission
Travel expenses should normally be submitted within 15 calendar days after the trip ends.
