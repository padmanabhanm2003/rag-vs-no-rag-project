Company Human Resources Policy
Version: 2026.3
Effective date: February 1, 2026

Performance Reviews
The company runs a formal performance review cycle once each calendar year. Managers are expected to conduct a review conversation with each direct report during the annual cycle.

New employees may receive an additional introductory review during their first six months.

Remote Work
Eligible employees may work remotely up to three days per week, subject to team requirements and manager approval.

Employee Records
Employees should keep their personal contact and emergency information current in the HR system.
