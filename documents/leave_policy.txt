Company Leave Policy
Version: 2026.2
Effective date: January 1, 2026

Parental Leave
Eligible full-time employees receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave following the birth or legal adoption of a child. The leave may be taken continuously or, subject to manager approval and applicable law, in separate blocks.

Employees should notify their manager and Human Resources at least 30 days before a planned leave when reasonably possible. In unexpected circumstances, notice should be provided as soon as practical.

Parental leave is separate from the standard annual leave balance. Employees should contact Human Resources for questions about eligibility, documentation, or coordination with other legally protected leave.

Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 paid sick days per calendar year.

Annual Leave
Employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
