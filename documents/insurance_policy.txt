Employee Health Insurance Policy
Version: 2026.1
Effective date: January 1, 2026

Medical Plan
The standard employee medical plan has an annual in-network individual deductible of USD 1,500. The family deductible is USD 3,000.

Preventive care is covered according to the plan's preventive-care provisions. Employees should review the plan summary for specific covered services.

Enrollment
New employees generally have 30 days from their employment start date to complete benefits enrollment.
