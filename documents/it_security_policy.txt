Company IT and Security Policy
Version: 2026.4
Effective date: April 1, 2026

Password Requirements
Employees must change their corporate password at least once every 90 days when the identity platform requires password rotation. Passwords must not be reused across unrelated personal services.

Multi-Factor Authentication
Multi-factor authentication is required for corporate applications that support it.

Lost or Stolen Devices
If a company laptop or mobile device is lost or stolen, the employee must immediately contact the IT Service Desk and Security team. The company may remotely lock or wipe managed devices.

Phishing
Employees must report suspected phishing messages through the company's security reporting mechanism and must not enter corporate credentials into suspicious links or forms.

Access
Employees must only access systems and documents necessary for their job responsibilities.
